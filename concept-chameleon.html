I build other people's brands. Seven of them are live right now.

What you are looking at is an unstyled HTML document — default typeface, default link colour, no decisions made. That is deliberate. On my own, there is nothing here to look at.

Keep scrolling. The page will become each client I have shipped for: their typeface, their colour, their shape language. Seven complete design systems, one after another.

Then it will go back to looking like this.

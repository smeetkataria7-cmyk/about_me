01Music

Keys and silence.

Self-taught, which mostly means years of getting things wrong slowly. Classical pieces, film scores, and improvisation that goes nowhere and everywhere at once. The piano is the one place the noise stops — no version control, no deploy, no one waiting on it.

02Road

Nowhere in particular.

Windows down at midnight with a playlist doing all the talking. The longest version of that was Leh to Nubra over Khardung La — 395 km on a Royal Enfield Classic 350, topping out at 17,500 feet where the air stops cooperating. The road is the one place that makes complete sense.

03Frames

Light and still moments.

Photographing what people walk straight past — moody skies, cracked walls, the good light hiding in a corner nobody looks at. Photography is just noticing, done slowly and on purpose. It is the closest thing I have to a design practice that isn't on a screen.
